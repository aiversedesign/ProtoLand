Information Overload
PSYCHOLOGY TIP

Information overload occurs when the amount of input exceeds our processing capacity, often resulting in a reduction in decision quality.

  • Help users decide
  • Less clutter = faster decisions
  • Eliminate unnecessary steps

Visual Hierarchy
PRODUCT TIP

Visual hierarchy is the order of elements on a page that determines which elements are perceived as most important.


If the hierarchy is not clear, the user will have to spend more time to understand the page.

  • Primary actions = most prominent
  • Storytelling guides user's journey
  • Clear hierarchy = faster decisions

Don't force users to think
#UX PRO TIP

People don't want to think, they want to book an appointment. Don't provide them all possible options, provide them limited ready-made slots.

  • How many days in advance do they book?
  • Do they book a month in advance?
  • How many time slots are available?

Users crave rewards
UX PRO TIP

Whether its unexpected and occasional rewards or bonuses for completing tasks, humans love rewards.


It keeps users engaged and motivated to complete the task.


In this case, giving free feed or showing a streak will motivate farm managers to keep inputting data daily; further increasing the engagement rate.
