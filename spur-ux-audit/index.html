Visual Hierarchy
PRODUCT TIP

Visual hierarchy is the order of elements on a page that determines which elements are perceived as most important.


If the hierarchy is not clear, the user will have to spend more time to understand the page.

  • Primary actions = most prominent
  • Storytelling guides user's journey
  • Clear hierarchy = faster decisions

Users crave rewards
UX PRO TIP

Whether its unexpected and occasional rewards or bonuses for completing tasks, humans love rewards.


It keeps users engaged and motivated to complete the task.


In this case, giving AI credits for completion motivated them to set up faster, further increasing the retention rate.
